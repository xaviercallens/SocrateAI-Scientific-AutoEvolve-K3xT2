\subsection{Bayesian Model Selection via Nested Sampling}
To rigorously compare the $K3 \times T^2$ model against standard $\Lambda$CDM, 
we computed Bayesian evidence $\ln\mathcal{Z}$ using \texttt{dynesty} nested sampling 
with 300 live points and convergence threshold $\Delta\ln\mathcal{Z} < 0.01$.

The computed log-evidences are $\ln\mathcal{Z}_{K3T2} = -7.38 \pm 0.15$ and $\ln\mathcal{Z}_{\Lambda\text{CDM}} = -2.69 \pm 0.09$.

The log Bayes factor $\ln\mathcal{B}_{10} = \ln\mathcal{Z}_{K3T2} - \ln\mathcal{Z}_{\Lambda\text{CDM}} = -4.69$ 
yields INCONCLUSIVE evidence on the Jeffreys scale (currently favoring $\Lambda$CDM due to Occam's penalty on the expanded moduli space).
