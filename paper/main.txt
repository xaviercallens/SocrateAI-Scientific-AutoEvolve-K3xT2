2.17). The Almkvist-Zudilin #1 surface is selected by an AI-driven evolutionary landscape
scan (AutoEvolve) of 12,000 candidate geometries over 300 generations, all verified by formal

high-S8 Planck/Euclid cosmology and does not resolve the S8 tension.
