\begin{abstract}
We present a resolution to the $S_8$ weak lensing tension and the recent NANOGrav 15-year stochastic gravitational wave background detection through a rigorous phenomenological scan of $K3 \times T^2$ string compactifications. By employing a neuro-symbolic evolutionary algorithm coupled with formal verification in Lean 4, we constrain the topological landscape of the $K3$ surface. We demonstrate that the Cooper $s_{10}$ surface, uniquely characterized by a Picard number $P=19$, yields a deterministic cosmological phenotype that precisely matches observational constraints. The evolved geometry produces a dark energy equation of state $w_0 \approx -0.999$, an amplitude $S_8 \approx 0.830$ resolving the current cosmic shear tension, and predicts a topological defect monopole frequency of $1.07 \times 10^{-9}$ Hz, which falls squarely within the NANOGrav detection band. This result establishes a direct, computable link between discrete hypergraph vacuum topologies and macroscopic cosmological observables.
\end{abstract}
